title: 'Beef bourguignon'
description: 'French beef stew braised slowly in red wine with bacon, mushrooms, carrots, and pearl onions, served with tender potatoes.'

cookingTime: 180

servings: 1

steps:
  - title: 'Brown the Bacon and Beef'
    actions:
      - 'Preheat the oven to 160°C (325°F).'
      - 'In a Dutch oven, cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp. Remove and set aside, leaving the fat.'
      - 'Pat the beef dry, season with salt and pepper, and toss in the flour.'
      - 'Brown the beef in the bacon fat in batches until well colored on all sides. Set aside.'
  - title: 'Build the Sauce'
    actions:
      - 'Add the carrot and onion to the pot and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute.'
      - 'Pour in the wine, scraping up the browned bits, then add the stock, thyme, and bay leaf.'
      - 'Return the beef and bacon to the pot and bring to a simmer.'
  - title: 'Braise'
    actions:
      - 'Cover and transfer to the oven for 2 1/2 hours, until the beef is fork-tender.'
      - 'Add the potatoes for the last hour of cooking.'
  - title: 'Cook the Mushrooms and Onions'
    actions:
      - 'Melt the butter in a frying pan and brown the pearl onions for 5 minutes.'
      - 'Add the mushrooms and cook until golden. Stir them into the stew.'
  - title: 'Finish and Serve'
    actions:
      - 'Remove the thyme and bay leaf. Simmer on the stove for a few minutes if the sauce needs thickening.'
      - 'Season to taste and serve sprinkled with parsley.'
