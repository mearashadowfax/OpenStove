title: 'Tom yum kung'

cookingTime: 30

servings: 1

steps:
  - title: 'Prepare the Aromatics'
    actions:
      - 'Cut the lemongrass into 5 cm pieces and bruise them with the back of a knife.'
      - 'Slice the galangal into thin rounds and tear the kaffir lime leaves, removing the center vein.'
      - 'Lightly crush the chilies.'
  - title: 'Make the Broth'
    actions:
      - 'Bring the stock to a boil in a small pot.'
      - 'Add the lemongrass, galangal, and lime leaves and simmer for 5 minutes until fragrant.'
  - title: 'Cook the Prawns'
    actions:
      - 'Add the mushrooms and tomato and simmer for 2 minutes.'
      - 'Stir in the chili paste, then add the prawns and cook for 2-3 minutes until just pink.'
  - title: 'Season and Serve'
    actions:
      - 'Take the pot off the heat and stir in the fish sauce, lime juice, and crushed chilies.'
      - 'Taste and balance: more lime for sour, fish sauce for salt, chili for heat.'
      - 'Serve at once, garnished with cilantro and lime slices.'
