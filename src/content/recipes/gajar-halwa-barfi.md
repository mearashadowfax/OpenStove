steps:
  - title: 'Cook the Carrots'
    actions:
      - 'Heat the ghee in a wide heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. Add the grated carrots and sauté for 8-10 minutes until they soften and lose their raw smell.'
      - 'Pour in the milk and bring to a boil, then lower the heat and cook, stirring often, until the milk is almost fully absorbed (about 30-35 minutes).'
  - title: 'Sweeten and Thicken'
    actions:
      - 'Add the sugar and stir; the mixture will loosen as the sugar melts.'
      - 'Keep cooking and stirring until it thickens again.'
      - 'Crumble in the milk powder and cook for 8-10 minutes until the mixture leaves the sides of the pan and comes together as one mass.'
      - 'Stir in the cardamom.'
  - title: 'Set the Barfi'
    actions:
      - 'Grease a 20 cm square tin with ghee and spread the mixture in evenly, pressing it flat.'
      - 'Scatter over the almonds and pistachios and press them in lightly.'
      - 'Leave to cool and set for 2 hours, then cut into squares.'
