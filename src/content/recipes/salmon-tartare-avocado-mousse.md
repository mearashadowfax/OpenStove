title: 'Salmon tartare with avocado mousse'
description: 'Fresh diced salmon dressed with lemon, shallot, and dill, layered over a silky avocado mousse and served with crisp toast.'

cookingTime: 20

servings: 1

steps:
  - title: 'Prepare the Salmon'
    actions:
      - 'Using a very sharp knife, cut the salmon into small 5 mm cubes and place in a chilled bowl.'
      - 'Add the shallot, capers, dill, olive oil, lemon zest, and half of the lemon juice.'
      - 'Season with salt and pepper, mix gently, and refrigerate while you make the mousse.'
  - title: 'Make the Avocado Mousse'
    actions:
      - 'Blend the avocado, cream cheese, remaining lemon juice, and a pinch of salt until completely smooth.'
  - title: 'Assemble'
    actions:
      - 'Place a ring mold in the center of a plate and spoon in the avocado mousse, smoothing the top.'
      - 'Top with the salmon tartare, press lightly, and carefully lift off the ring.'
      - 'Garnish with microgreens and serve with lemon wedges and toasted bread.'
