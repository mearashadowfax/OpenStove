steps:
  - title: 'Marinate the Chicken'
    actions:
      - 'Mix the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, lemon juice, and salt in a bowl.'
      - 'Add the chicken, coat well, cover, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or overnight).'
  - title: 'Fry the Onions'
    actions:
      - 'Heat the oil or ghee in a heavy pot over medium heat and fry the sliced onion until deep golden and crisp, 12-15 minutes.'
      - 'Lift out half of the onions onto a paper towel for garnish and leave the rest in the pot.'
  - title: 'Par-cook the Rice'
    actions:
      - 'Rinse the basmati until the water runs clear, then soak it for 20 minutes.'
      - 'Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil with the bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon.'
      - 'Add the drained rice and boil for 5-6 minutes until about 70% cooked. Drain well.'
      - 'Warm the milk and stir in the saffron to bloom.'
  - title: 'Cook the Chicken'
    actions:
      - 'Add the marinated chicken to the pot with the onions and cook over medium-high heat for 10 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and the chicken is nearly cooked.'
  - title: 'Layer and Steam'
    actions:
      - 'Spread the rice evenly over the chicken. Drizzle with the saffron milk and scatter over the mint, cilantro, and half of the reserved onions.'
      - 'Cover tightly with foil and a lid, then cook on the lowest heat for 20-25 minutes.'
      - 'Rest for 5 minutes, then gently fold from the bottom and serve topped with the remaining crispy onions.'
